Our approach

We are not just making nutrition products. We are building a clearer nutrition experience.

Braymil draws on Nutribray’s focus on science, quality, human needs and nutrition expertise, translating those principles into an approachable consumer-facing range.

That means helping families understand what a product is for, who it is intended for and when professional guidance matters—without making the experience feel complicated.

✓Products organised around age, stage and nutritional need.
✓Specialised nutrition separated clearly from everyday stage-based ranges.
✓Educational content designed to support informed conversations with healthcare professionals.
What guides us

Four values behind the brand.

These principles shape how Braymil approaches nutrition, product information and the experience we create for families and professionals.

Science-Based

Nutrition decisions should be rooted in evidence, product purpose and clear formulation logic.

Quality First

Quality is treated as a system—from formulation and production discipline to information and presentation.

Human-Centered

Nutrition should be easier for parents and caregivers to understand at every life stage.

Global Outlook

A broader view of nutrition helps the brand keep learning, improving and responding to evolving needs.
